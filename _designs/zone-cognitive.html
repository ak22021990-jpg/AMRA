REFERENCE DOCUMENT // OP-PROC-992

Fraud Pattern Arbitration

When a transaction is flagged under condition Alpha-7, the reviewing associate must immediately cross-reference the user's last known IP address. If the distance between the IP's geolocation and the shipping address exceeds 500 miles, the associate must initiate protocol Beta-Void.

However, if the account holds "Trusted Tier" status (defined as having over 10 successful transactions in the past 6 months) AND the purchase total is under $250, Beta-Void is superseded by Gamma-Hold.

Under no circumstances should the associate directly contact the user if Beta-Void is active. Conversely, Gamma-Hold requires a minimum of two outreach attempts within a 24-hour window before final block placement.
